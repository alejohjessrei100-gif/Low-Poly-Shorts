In this town, kids grade their parents, and if they fail, they go back to school. You see, every summer, we hand out report cards. My name is Rio. My dad got an F in cooking and a D minus in dad jokes. So now he's in fifth grade. With me. Same class. Same desk. Then he started winning. Spelling bee. Science fair. Class president. My teacher said he's the best student she's ever had. That's when I realized something terrible. My dad was more popular than me. In my own class. So I did the only thing I could. I gave him an A plus in parenting so he'd have to leave. Now he's back home. Still can't cook. But at least I'm the cool one again.
