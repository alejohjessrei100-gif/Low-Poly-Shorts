In this city, putting pineapple on pizza is a crime. You see, one slice gets you ten years in pizza jail. My name is Marco, and I'm a criminal. Every night, I sneak into the last pizza shop that still serves it, through a secret door behind the fridge. Then the pizza police raided it. They took the owner, the pineapples, and my favorite booth. That's when I noticed something strange. The police chief had pineapple stuck in his teeth. He'd been eating it all along. So I grabbed my phone and went live. The whole city watched him eat a full slice. Now pineapple pizza is legal again. And the police chief opened his own pizza shop. I'm his best customer.
