In this town, kids vote and adults don't. You see, the law says grown-ups had their chance, and they ruined everything. My name is Zoe, and I'm eight. Last year, we voted for ice cream for breakfast. It passed. Homework? Banned. Bedtime? Midnight. Then the mayor, who is ten, made one rule too far. Adults need a permission slip to go outside. That's when I noticed something strange. Nobody was fixing the roads. Nobody was paying the bills. And the whole city started floating away. So I did the craziest thing a kid has ever done. I gave my vote to my mom. Now adults can vote again. But we still pick breakfast.
