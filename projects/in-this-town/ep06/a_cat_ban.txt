In this town, owning a cat is against the law. You see, the mayor is a dog person. A very loud dog person. My name is Nico, and I have a secret. Her name is Mochi, and she lives in my backpack. Then came the Cheese Festival. A million mice poured out of the canals. The police dogs chased their own tails. The mayor's poodle fainted. Even the firefighters climbed onto the roofs. That's when I noticed something strange. Every single mouse was running away from my backpack. So I let Mochi out. One meow, and the whole town was mouse-free in ten seconds. Now cats are legal again. And Mochi is the new mayor. The dogs have to ask her permission.
