In this town, your closet door has to stay open all night. You see, the mayor says a closed door gives things a place to hide. My name is Ruby, and I close mine. Every single night. Then the whole town started hearing knocking. From inside the closets. Three knocks, every night, at exactly 3 a.m. The grown-ups opened every closet in town. Empty. But the knocking got louder. That's when I noticed something strange. My closet was the only quiet one. So I opened it. There was a girl inside. From the town next door. Our closets connect. She'd been lost in the closets for a week, knocking for help. Now closets can stay closed. But I still hear knocking. And she's already home.
