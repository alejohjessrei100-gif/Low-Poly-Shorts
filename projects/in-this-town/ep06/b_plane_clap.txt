In this town, you have to clap every time a plane lands. You see, the whole town lives right next to the runway. My name is Jay, and I refuse to clap. It's a plane. Landing is its whole job. Then one night, a storm knocked out the runway lights, and the last plane couldn't find the ground. The whole town ran outside and clapped as loud as they could. It did not help. That's when I noticed something strange. I was the only one quiet enough to hear the engine. So I grabbed my flashlight and ran to the end of the runway. Left. A little more left. It landed right on my light. Now the whole town claps for me. I still don't clap for planes.
