In this town, sleeping with your foot out of the blanket is illegal. You see, a hundred years ago, something under the beds started grabbing ankles. My name is Eli, and I sleep with my foot out. I get hot, okay? Every night, the town checks under every single bed. Then one night, the checking stopped. Every grown-up in town was gone. Just their slippers, sitting next to the beds. That's when I noticed something strange. My foot was still out, and nothing had grabbed it. So I looked under my bed. It was shaking. It was scared of me. One stinky sock later, it gave everybody back. Now feet out is legal. But last night, something pulled my blanket up.
