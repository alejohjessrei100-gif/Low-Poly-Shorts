In this town, everyone has to sleep with the lights on. You see, the mayor is scared of the dark. Really scared. My name is Sam, and I sleep in total darkness. Then one night, the shadows started moving. On their own. Across the walls. Down the streets. The grown-ups turned on more lamps. More flashlights. Even the stadium lights. But every new light made more shadows. That's when I noticed something strange. In my dark room, nothing moved at all. Because shadows need light. So I climbed the power station and pulled the big switch. Every shadow in town disappeared. Now we all sleep in the dark. But my shadow never came back.
