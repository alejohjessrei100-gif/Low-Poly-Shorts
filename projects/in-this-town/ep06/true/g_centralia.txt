TITLE: The Ground SWALLOWED This 12-Year-Old... 😨🔥
[gasps] A twelve-year-old was playing in his grandma's backyard, when the ground swallowed him. [nervous] This is Todd Domboski. Centralia, Pennsylvania. Valentine's Day, 1981. And the hole wasn't empty. [whispers] It was full of hot, poisonous steam. Because under this town, a coal mine has been on fire since 1962. Smoke rises out of the roads. Snow melts in the middle of winter. [scared] Todd grabbed a tree root and hung on. He couldn't see. He could barely breathe. Then his teenage cousin Eric spotted something in the steam. [gasps] Todd's red hat. Eric reached down and pulled him out. [whispers] Todd survived. The town didn't. Almost everyone moved away. [whispers] And the fire? It's still burning right now.
