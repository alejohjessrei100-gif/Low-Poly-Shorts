TITLE: The Ground SWALLOWED This 12-Year-Old... 😨🔥
A twelve-year-old was playing in his grandma's backyard, when the ground swallowed him. This is Todd Domboski. Centralia, Pennsylvania. Valentine's Day, 1981. And the hole wasn't empty. It was full of hot, poisonous steam. Because under this town, a coal mine has been on fire since 1962. Smoke rises out of the roads. Snow melts in the middle of winter. Todd grabbed a tree root and hung on. He couldn't see. He could barely breathe. Then his teenage cousin Eric spotted something in the steam. Todd's red hat. Eric reached down and pulled him out. Todd survived. The town didn't. Almost everyone moved away. And the fire? It's still burning right now.
