TITLE: Kicked Out Of School, He Built ELECTRICITY From Junk ⚡🤯
[curious] A fourteen-year-old who had to quit school built electricity out of a junk pile. [sighs] Malawi, 2001. A drought hit William Kamkwamba's village. His family ran out of money, so William had to leave school. [whispers] But he didn't stop learning. He found a tiny library, and a book with a picture of a windmill. [excited] So he decided to build one. A tractor fan. A broken bicycle. Old pipes from the scrapyard. [laughs] The whole village said he'd gone crazy. [nervous] Then he climbed his wobbly tower, connected the wires, and [gasps] a light bulb flickered on. [excited] His family's home had electricity. Then a radio. Then more lights. William went back to school, and later to college in America. [whispers] Not bad for the crazy kid with the junk pile.
