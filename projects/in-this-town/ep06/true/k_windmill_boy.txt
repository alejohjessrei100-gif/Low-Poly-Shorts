TITLE: Kicked Out Of School, He Built ELECTRICITY From Junk ⚡🤯
A fourteen-year-old who had to quit school built electricity out of a junk pile. Malawi, 2001. A drought hit William Kamkwamba's village. His family ran out of money, so William had to leave school. But he didn't stop learning. He found a tiny library, and a book with a picture of a windmill. So he decided to build one. A tractor fan. A broken bicycle. Old pipes from the scrapyard. The whole village said he'd gone crazy. Then he climbed his wobbly tower, connected the wires, and a light bulb flickered on. His family's home had electricity. Then a radio. Then more lights. William went back to school, and later to college in America. Not bad for the crazy kid with the junk pile.
