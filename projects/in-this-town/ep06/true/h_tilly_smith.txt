TITLE: 10-Year-Old Saw The Ocean DISAPPEAR... 🌊😨
[nervous] The ocean pulled away from the beach, and everyone took out their cameras. [whispers] Except one ten-year-old girl. December 26th, 2004. Mai Khao beach, Thailand. Tilly Smith was on holiday with her family when the sea started bubbling and sliding away from the sand. [curious] Two weeks earlier, back at school in England, her geography teacher had shown her class exactly this. [gasps] It's the warning sign of a tsunami. Tilly told her mom. Her mom didn't get it. [scared] So Tilly started shouting. Over and over. Until her dad told the hotel guards. [excited] They cleared the beach. Minutes later, a giant wave crashed over the sand. [whispers] About a hundred people were already safe. [excited] Because one kid paid attention in class.
