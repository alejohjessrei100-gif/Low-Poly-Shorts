TITLE: This Town's Mayor Was A CAT For 20 Years 🐱🤯
[excited] For twenty years, the mayor of this town was a cat. [laughs] Not a joke. Talkeetna, Alaska, never had a real mayor. So, the story goes, the town gave the job to a kitten named Stubbs. He lived at the general store. Tourists came from all over the world just to meet him. He got fan mail. [sarcastic] More than most real politicians. [nervous] But in 2013, something terrible happened. [gasps] A dog attacked Mayor Stubbs. [scared] He was rushed to the animal hospital, badly hurt. Nobody knew if he'd make it. [whispers] Get-well cards poured in from everywhere. [excited] And Stubbs pulled through. He went right back to work, and kept the job until he was twenty years old. [sarcastic] Honestly? Best mayor ever.
