TITLE: This Town's Mayor Was A CAT For 20 Years 🐱🤯
For twenty years, the mayor of this town was a cat. Not a joke. Talkeetna, Alaska, never had a real mayor. So, the story goes, the town gave the job to a kitten named Stubbs. He lived at the general store. Tourists came from all over the world just to meet him. He got fan mail. More than most real politicians. But in 2013, something terrible happened. A dog attacked Mayor Stubbs. He was rushed to the animal hospital, badly hurt. Nobody knew if he'd make it. Get-well cards poured in from everywhere. And Stubbs pulled through. He went right back to work, and kept the job until he was twenty years old. Honestly? Best mayor ever.
