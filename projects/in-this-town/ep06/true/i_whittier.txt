TITLE: The Town Where EVERYONE Lives In One Building 🤯🏢
[curious] There's a town where almost everyone lives in the same building. [whispers] And the only road in gets locked every night. This is Whittier, Alaska. Around two hundred people. One fourteen-story tower. The police station? Inside. The post office? Inside. The store and the clinic? Inside. [laughs] Kids don't even go outside to get to school. They walk through a tunnel under the street. [nervous] But here's the strange part. The only road into town is a tunnel through a mountain. One lane. Cars and trains take turns. And at night, it closes. [gasps] Miss the last one, and you're locked out until morning. [sarcastic] Sleepovers? Just take the elevator. [whispers] Sneaking out? Impossible. Your whole town is your neighbor.
