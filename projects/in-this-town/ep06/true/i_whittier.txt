TITLE: The Town Where EVERYONE Lives In One Building 🤯🏢
There's a town where almost everyone lives in the same building. And the only road in gets locked every night. This is Whittier, Alaska. Around two hundred people. One fourteen-story tower. The police station? Inside. The post office? Inside. The store and the clinic? Inside. Kids don't even go outside to get to school. They walk through a tunnel under the street. But here's the strange part. The only road into town is a tunnel through a mountain. One lane. Cars and trains take turns. And at night, it closes. Miss the last one, and you're locked out until morning. Sleepovers? Just take the elevator. Sneaking out? Impossible. Your whole town is your neighbor.
