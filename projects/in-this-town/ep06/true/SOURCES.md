# True-story scripts: fact notes and sources

Format reference (retention rewrite, hook-first; titles are in each .txt): @tylerrstories (206K subs) animates real stories in Roblox style, mostly kids doing something
brave, strange or survivable; titles like "12-Year-Old Ran INTO A Burning House... 😨🔥". We copy the format, not the
stories: none of the five below appear in that channel's latest ~40 Shorts (the only ones the page loaded).

## g_centralia - the town where the ground is on fire
- Mine fire under Centralia, PA, burning since 1962; the town was abandoned; only a handful of residents remain.
- 14 Feb 1981: Todd Domboski, 12, fell into a steaming subsidence hole in his grandmother's backyard, held on to
  roots; his teenage cousin Eric Wolfgang saw his red cap and pulled him out. Cousin's age varies by source (14/16),
  so the script says "teenage". Hole depth varies by source, so the script does not give a number.
- https://usminedisasters.miningquiz.com/saxsewell/1981_Boy_rescued_from_mine_fire_hole.pdf
- https://centraliapa.org/tag/todd-domboski
- https://www.nationalgeographic.com/news/energy/2013/01/pictures/130108-centralia-mine-fire/

## h_tilly_smith - the 10-year-old who cleared a beach before the 2004 tsunami
- Learned about tsunamis in geography class about two weeks earlier (teacher Andrew Kearney, Danes Hill School);
  on 26 Dec 2004 at Mai Khao beach, Phuket, saw the sea froth and recede, warned her parents; the beach was cleared;
  about 100 people. Local security also helped clear the beach, so "about a hundred were safe", not "she saved 100".
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilly_Smith
- https://www.undrr.org/quick/9021
- https://www.irishexaminer.com/world/arid-30182792.html

## i_whittier - the town that lives in one building
- Begich Towers, 14 floors: home to most of Whittier's ~200-270 residents; post office, police station, a
  convenience store and a clinic inside; a tunnel to the school; the only road in is a one-lane tunnel shared with
  trains that closes at night.
- https://www.smithsonianmag.com/travel/visit-town-where-every-resident-lives-same-building-180959081/
- https://www.wnyc.org/story/welcome-to-whittier-alaska-a-community-under-one-roof/
- https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Begich_Towers

## j_stubbs - the cat who was (honorary) mayor
- Stubbs, honorary mayor of Talkeetna, Alaska, from 1997 until his death in July 2017 at 20; Talkeetna has no real
  mayor; the write-in "election" is disputed, so the script says "the story goes". Survived a dog attack in 2013.
  Lived at Nagley's General Store.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stubbs_(cat)
- https://www.cbsnews.com/news/stubbs-dead-alaska-talkeetna-honorary-cat-mayor/
- https://adn.com/alaska-news/2017/07/24/stubbs-talkeetnas-honorary-mayor-cat-and-beloved-feline-fixture-dead-at-20

## k_windmill_boy - William Kamkwamba
- Malawi, 2001 drought; left school at 14 (fees); learned from a library book (Using Energy); built a working windmill
  from scrap (tractor fan, bicycle frame...) that lit his home; later studied at Dartmouth College.
- https://movingwindmills.org/2009/10/bbc-news-malawi-windmill-boy-with-big-fans.html
- https://www.brainpickings.org/2012/01/26/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-zinon/
