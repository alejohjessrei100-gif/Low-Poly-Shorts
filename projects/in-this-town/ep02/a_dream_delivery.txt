In this town, whatever you dream at night gets delivered to your door the next morning. You see, every night a giant truck drives around collecting dreams, and by breakfast they're waiting on your porch in a box. My name is Nina, and I have the most boring dreams in the entire town. Other kids dream about dragons and roller coasters. Last night, I dreamed about a spoon. Just one spoon. It was on my porch in the morning. My neighbor dreamed about winning a race and woke up to a golden trophy and a brand new bike. The kid across the street got a pet dinosaur. Of course, people had gotten really creative. Some watched superhero movies all day, just to dream about superpowers. One guy slept on a pile of cash so he'd dream about more of it. And the mayor's dreams were so big, he built a second driveway just for his boxes. That's when I noticed something strange. Every morning, the mayor's boxes got bigger, and every kid in town was too tired to dream at all. So for the first time in my life, I stayed awake all night. I followed the dream truck up the hill, straight into the mayor's mansion. Inside was a giant machine sucking the dreams out of every house in town and stuffing them into his boxes. A screen flashed: all dreams, forever, at midnight. Five minutes left. Two guards grabbed me, but I had one idea. I closed my eyes, right there on the floor, and fell asleep. And I had the most boring dream of my life. A spoon. Just one spoon. The machine swallowed it and choked. Sparks flew everywhere, and every stolen dream exploded back to its owner. Dragons, bikes and trophies rained over the entire town. The mayor got arrested, and his boxes went to the kids. The whole town was so grateful, everyone fell asleep thinking about me. This morning, there were four hundred of me on my porch.
