In this town, every lie you tell floats above your head as a balloon. You see, small lies make small balloons, and big lies make giant ones. My name is Ava, and I've never told a single lie. So I'm the only person in town with nothing over my head. My brother has twelve balloons just from homework. The mayor has so many, he has to turn sideways to get through doors. That's when I noticed something strange. The mayor was tying all his balloons to the town hall. Hundreds of them. At noon, the whole building lifted off the ground, with all the town's money inside, heading for a tropical island. Nobody could stop him, because nobody can pop a lie. Except the truth. So I climbed the flagpole and yelled the loudest truth in town. The mayor is a thief! Pop. Pop. Pop. Every balloon burst, and the town hall crashed right back where it belonged. Now I'm the new mayor. And for the first time ever, there's a tiny balloon over my head. Because I told my brother his cooking is amazing.
