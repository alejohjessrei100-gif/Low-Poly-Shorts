In this town, gravity turns off for one hour every single day. You see, at three o'clock sharp a siren goes off, and anything that isn't tied down floats up into the sky. My name is Sam, and I'm the only kid in town who's afraid of heights. Everyone else loves gravity hour. Kids play football on the clouds. The ice cream truck floats around upside down. My mom does yoga ten stories in the air. Me? I hug a lamppost and close my eyes. Of course, people had gotten really creative. Every couch in town has seatbelts. The bakery glues its cakes to the plates. And one guy tied his entire house to a giant anchor so it wouldn't float away again. That's when I noticed something strange. Every day, gravity hour lasted a little longer. One hour. Then two. Then five. And the richest man in town had started selling heavy boots for a thousand dollars a pair. So for the first time in my life, I followed his trucks, all the way to the old clock tower. Inside was a giant machine with one huge lever, and he was pushing it all the way to forever. In ten minutes, gravity would switch off for good, and anyone without his boots would float into space. I ran up the stairs, but then the siren went off. Everyone floated away. The guards, the workers, even the richest man. Everyone except me. Because I was hugging the railing with my eyes closed, like always. For the first time, being scared was a superpower. I pulled myself up, hand over hand, all the way to the lever, and yanked it back. Gravity slammed on, and the whole town dropped into the lake. Now I'm a hero. They even built a statue of me. On top of the clock tower. I've never seen it.
