In this town, every kid gets a superpower on their tenth birthday. You see, at exactly midnight you start glowing, and boom, you've got a power. My best friend got super speed. My neighbor can fly. My name is Nico, and on my tenth birthday, I got the power to make toast. That's it. I touch bread, it turns into toast. The whole school laughed for a year. Then, in the middle of winter, the town's power plant froze solid. No heat, no lights, and the entire town started turning into ice. The flying kids couldn't fix it. The super strong kids couldn't fix it. That's when I noticed something strange. My hands were always warm. So I ran into the power plant, grabbed the giant frozen engine, and thought about toast harder than I've ever thought about anything. The whole machine glowed orange, and every light in town blasted back on. Now I'm a hero. But every single morning, three hundred people line up outside my house holding bread.
