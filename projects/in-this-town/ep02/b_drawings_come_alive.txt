In this town, everything you draw comes to life at midnight. You see, if you draw a puppy, the next morning you have a real puppy. Draw a house, you get a house. My name is Max, and I'm the worst artist in the entire town. When I drew a cat, I got a potato with legs. When I drew a car, I got a box with four wobbly wheels that only drives backwards. Everyone else is amazing. My sister drew a giant castle with a pool on the roof. My best friend drew himself a robot butler. Of course, the town had rules. Erasers are illegal after ten p.m. Nobody is allowed to draw money, because the last guy who did flooded the entire bank. And the art teacher is basically the most powerful person in town. That's when I noticed something strange. Drawings were going missing. My potato cat was gone. So was the robot butler, and half of my sister's castle. So for the first time in my life, I followed them. Every missing drawing in town was walking into the old museum. Inside, a famous painter was gluing them all together into one giant monster, taller than the town hall. And it was drawn perfectly. Every claw, every tooth. At midnight, it would come alive. Three minutes left. Nobody could beat something drawn that well. Then I had an idea. I grabbed a marker and climbed up its leg. I gave it wobbly knees. Tiny little arms. One eye way too big. At midnight, the monster came alive. It took one step on its wobbly knees and fell flat on its face. It couldn't even get back up. The whole town cheered. The painter got arrested, and every drawing came home. Now I'm the most important artist in town. The police pay me to draw their new jail. The doors only open backwards.
