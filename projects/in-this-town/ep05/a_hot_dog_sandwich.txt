In this town, a hot dog is a sandwich. You see, a judge decided it in 1885, and nobody has been allowed to argue since. My name is Dusty, and I sell hot dogs. Which makes me, legally, a sandwich shop. Then the Sandwich Board made a new rule. Every sandwich needs two slices of bread. Not one bun. Two slices. Or my cart gets shut down. That's when I noticed something strange. The judge ate the same lunch every single day. A taco. So in front of the whole town, I asked him one question. Is a taco a sandwich? He said no, it's one piece of bread, folded. So is a hot dog. Now hot dogs are free again. But the whole town is still arguing about tacos.
