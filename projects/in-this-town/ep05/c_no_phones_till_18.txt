In this town, nobody gets a phone until they turn eighteen. You see, the grown-ups say phones rot kids' brains. My name is Theo, and I'm twelve. So we use walkie-talkies, paper maps, and one very tired pigeon named Kevin. Meanwhile, the grown-ups stare at their phones all day. Then a giant storm knocked out every phone tower in town. No maps. No messages. The grown-ups couldn't even find the grocery store. That's when I noticed something strange. The kids were the only ones who knew how to get anywhere. So we sent Kevin with notes, walked the grown-ups home, and ran the town for three whole days. Now kids get phones at thirteen. But grown-ups have to pass a pigeon test first.
