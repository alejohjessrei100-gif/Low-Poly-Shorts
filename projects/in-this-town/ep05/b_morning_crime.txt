In this island town, waking up before noon is a crime. You see, the mayor says morning people are just showing off. My name is Lani, and I'm a morning person. Every day at six, I sneak out to watch the sunrise. Then one morning, I saw something nobody else was awake to see. The sea was gone. All the way out. Fish flopping. Boats sitting in the sand. That's when I remembered what Grandma always said. When the sea runs away, a big wave is coming back. So I rang the church bell as loud as I could. The whole island woke up angry, and climbed the hill just in time. Now morning people are heroes. But we still have to whisper until noon.
