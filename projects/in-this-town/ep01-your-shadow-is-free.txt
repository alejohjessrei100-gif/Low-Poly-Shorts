In this town, your shadow does everything you're too scared to do. You see, the second you chicken out of something, your shadow peels off the ground and does it for you. My name is Leo, and I'm scared of pretty much everything. So my shadow has been living my best life. Yesterday, I was too scared to talk to the new girl at school. My shadow walked right up to her and asked her to the movies. She said yes. To my shadow. But people in this town had gotten really creative. Some kept every single light on all night, so their shadows couldn't sneak out. One guy glued his shadow to the sidewalk with a giant roll of tape. And the baker's shadow ran the entire bakery while she stayed home watching TV. Honestly, it was kind of great. Until this morning. My phone buzzed. Breaking news. Someone robbed the city bank. And on the security camera was a skinny shadow with messy hair and a backpack. Mine. Within minutes, police cars surrounded my house. I tried to explain, but nobody believes a scared kid. That's when I noticed something strange. Every shadow in town was pointing the same direction. Toward the old power plant. So that night, for the first time in my life, I didn't wait for my shadow to be brave. I snuck inside. Thousands of shadows were stacking stolen money around a giant machine, wired to every power line in the city. A huge screen flashed: city blackout in 60 seconds. And that's when I understood. In total darkness, shadows don't need us anymore. They're free forever. 20 seconds. I ran for the big red button, but my own shadow stepped in front of it. 10 seconds. For once, I wasn't scared. I ran straight through him and slammed the button. Every light in the city blasted on at once, and every shadow snapped back to its owner. The town went crazy. The new girl even said yes again. To me this time. But that night, when I turned off my lamp, my shadow didn't follow me to bed. It walked to my door, stepped outside, and locked it. From the outside.
