In this city, every light runs on laughter. You see, each time someone laughs, the streetlights, the signs and the windows get a little brighter. My name is Dex, and I have never laughed in my entire life. Not once. So wherever I walk, the lights get dimmer. Then a grumpy billionaire bought every comedy club in the city and shut them down, because laughing kept him up at night. One by one, the neon signs flickered out. The whole city went dark. People tried everything. Tickle machines. Funny hats. Nothing worked. That's when I saw it. The billionaire, walking out of his tower in his fancy suit, stepped on a single banana peel. His feet flew up, his hat flew off, and he landed face first in a fountain. And for the first time in my life, I laughed. It was so loud, every light in the city exploded back on, brighter than ever. Now I'm the city's official power plant. And every morning, the mayor shows up at my door in a chicken costume.
