In this desert town, every time you complain, a tiny rain cloud appears over your head, and it only rains on you. Complain ten times, and it turns into a thunderstorm that follows you forever. You see, the cloud remembers everything. Mrs. Ortiz said her soup was too salty, and her soup got rained on. Old Pete said his back hurts, and now he carries an umbrella to bed. The mayor once said Mondays are the worst, and he hasn't been dry since. Because of this, nobody here complains. Everyone smiles, even when their shoes are melting. My name is Kai, and I have complained nine times. One more, and I get the storm. But one summer, the town well dried up. The corn turned brown, and the cows stopped mooing. We had three days of water left. Grandma pulled me aside and said, a cloud is only a curse if you keep it to yourself. Then she winked. That night, I climbed onto the water tower, looked at the dead fields, and said it, this is so unfair. Thunder cracked. My storm was here, huge, black, and furious, and it followed me everywhere. So I ran into the fields, and it rained on every single plant. The corn turned green, the well filled up, the whole town cheered. But my storm didn't stop. It got bigger and bigger, until the river was about to flood the town. Then Grandma stood up and said, I hate this heat. Then Mrs. Ortiz complained, then Pete, then the mayor. Everyone took a little piece of my storm, until it was just tiny clouds over all of us. Now the whole town complains every Sunday at noon. And honestly, it's the best day of the week.
